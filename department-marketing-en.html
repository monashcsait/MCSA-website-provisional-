Our core teams

External Team

Build partnerships that bring opportunities to students.

  • Approach businesses in dining, entertainment, education and technology to negotiate student offers.
  • Plan in-person talks that connect students with career and learning resources.
  • Seek sponsorship for MCSA events and maintain lasting partnerships.

Internal Team

Make student benefits visible through words, social media and design.

  • Write promotional articles and advertising copy for MCSA and its partners.
  • Develop engaging campaigns around campus life and relevant trends.
  • Create posters, videos and other visual content that makes promotions engaging.

Roles and requirements

01

Experience is not essential. Initiative, responsibility and a willingness to act matter.

02

Negotiation, design and marketing writing can all be learned. Bring an eye for presentation, communication skills and the courage to try.

External Team

Requirements

  • Take initiative, participate regularly in promotional activities and develop business contacts.
  • Communicate partnership value clearly and build long-term relationships.
  • Understand business needs and propose mutually beneficial partnerships.
  • Stay resilient when facing rejection, and keep refining your approach.
  • Work with other departments to deliver partnerships effectively.

Internal Team

Requirements

  • Write engaging articles and advertising copy.
  • Spot relevant trends and develop creative content connected to campus life.
  • Understand platforms such as WeChat, Xiaohongshu and Instagram.
  • Basic design or layout skills, including Canva or Photoshop, are a plus.
  • Use feedback and performance data to improve content strategy.
