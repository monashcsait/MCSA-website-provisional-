Our core teams

Alumni Events

From an idea to a real encounter

  • Help plan and deliver Melbourne alumni gatherings and larger alumni receptions in China.
  • Develop themes and schedules, liaise with alumni and guests, and coordinate invitations and delivery.
  • Review each event and capture lessons for the next one.

Alumni Communications

Bring worthwhile stories to more people

  • Run alumni communities, share event information, respond to needs and encourage conversation.
  • Plan event articles and recaps, and maintain accounts on platforms such as Xiaohongshu.
  • Contribute video editing, visual design and event coverage, including alumni series such as “友说远川”.

Ways to contribute

Figures from the recruitment material: over 20 alumni communities connecting 3,000+ alumni.

Alumni Events

Helpful experience and qualities

  • Experience in event planning, project delivery or teamwork helps you get started.
  • Be dependable and attentive, manage your time and take ownership of tasks.
  • Be willing to learn organisation and planning through practical projects.

Alumni Communications

Helpful experience and qualities

  • Experience in social media, community management or content creation is a plus.
  • Bring an eye for content, an understanding of online audiences and reliable follow-through.
  • Commit to ongoing teamwork and developing the alumni community’s voice.

A semester with us

01

Plan the next alumni event together, from its theme to everyone’s responsibilities, and help online acquaintances meet in person.

02

Keep alumni communities active, share events and useful information, and listen to what members need.

03

Interview alumni, document events and create articles and videos while learning about work, industries and everyday life.

04

Celebrate over dinner, study together during finals, and make time for cooking, board games and conversation. Gain experience and friendships through university.

What you can gain

Practical experience

Build projects and content you can discuss in your CV and portfolio.

Industry perspectives

Talk with alumni in HR, technology, finance and media about CVs, further study and careers. Their experience offers perspectives rather than a single right answer.

More ways to contribute

Meet like-minded friends through projects, dinners, board games and everyday conversations.
